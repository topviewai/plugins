## Shared specification

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide 16:9 homepage card cover, edge-to-edge finished artwork. Style: sophisticated analog editorial collage, vintage cinema moodboard on warm ivory visibly textured paper. Photographic black-and-white halftone cutouts with imperfect scissor-cut edges, subtle photocopy grain and tactile paper shadows. Restricted palette: ivory, charcoal black, a few energetic vermilion-red hand-drawn grease-pencil circles and swooping arrows. High contrast central subject, asymmetrical supporting photo scraps, thoughtful negative space. NOT vector, NOT 3D icons, NOT purple illustration, NOT anime, NOT web UI or framed screenshot. No text, no words, no logo, no watermark. Make the subject understandable at a 330px-wide thumbnail. Keep main subject safely inside central 80% of wide image.
